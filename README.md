# ⚡ Electric Vehicle EDA & Visualization

Analyze U.S. electric vehicle adoption trends by county from 2010 to 2020, including EV population growth, BEV vs PHEV distribution, top counties, and EV-to-total-vehicles ratios. 📈

## 📊 Overview

This project analyzes the **Electric Vehicle Population Size History by County** dataset to:

1. Track total EV population growth over time
2. Compare BEV vs PHEV distribution
3. Identify top 10 counties by EV total and EV ratio
4. Examine county-level EV-to-total-vehicle ratio trends

## 📝 Article

You can access the full article here: [Powering the Future: An Analytical Journey Through the 2024 Electric Vehicle Population Data with Python](https://anpabelt.medium.com/powering-the-future-an-analytical-journey-through-the-2024-electric-vehicle-population-data-with-9df58b954c46) 📝
